# Spartanburg Presbyterian polling-site labels

The county's [polling-site material](https://www.spartanburgcounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/106382/Mt-Calvary-Presbyterian) identifies Mt. Calvary Presbyterian, and its [church site](https://www.mtcalvary.org/what-we-believe) identifies PCA. The map labels the site `Mt. Calvary Presbyterian Church, PCA`.

[Trinity Presbyterian's history](https://www.trinityspartanburg.com/about-trinity) says it left PCA in 2019 and is now part of Evangel Presbytery. The map labels it `Trinity Presbyterian Church, Evangel Presbytery`. The county's [polling list](https://www.spartanburgcounty.org/DocumentCenter/View/105619/Polling-Places-with-Map-Link?bidId=) gives its Oak Grove Road address, matching the congregation.

The labels are applied by `scripts/build_sc_precinct_friendly_names.js` to precinct geometry, centroids, and every friendly-name code alias.
